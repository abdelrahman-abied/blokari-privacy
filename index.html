Blokari Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you play Blokari (package com.amacraft.blokari), a puzzle game for Android made by amacraft ("we", "us").

In short

  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device. We have no server and no account system, and we do not receive your data.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show and measure ads, as described below. In the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent first.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Sharing only happens when you tap Share, through your phone's own share sheet.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following in its private storage on your phone, so you can continue where you left off:

  • scores, best scores, your saved game, levels, stars, puzzles solved, achievements and stickers;
  • coins, power-ups, unlocked themes, missions, daily rewards, streaks and season progress;
  • the recent friend challenges you sent or opened (the number that sets the piece order, the scores and the date);
  • your settings: language, light or dark mode, sound, colour marks, left-handed layout and reminder preferences.

This information never leaves your device to reach us. You can delete all of it by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the game. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this data in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account, and we cannot access it.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

Blokari is free and is paid for by ads: banners, full-screen ads between games, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for coins or other in-game items. The ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited in the EEA and UK).

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game collects and sends to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (city or region, not your precise location);
  • device and app information, such as the device model, operating system, language and app version;
  • interactions with ads, such as ads shown, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information about how the app and the ad SDK perform.

We do not see this data in a form that identifies you; we only receive summary reports from AdMob (for example, how many ads were shown). Google uses this data as described in its policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>
  • Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
  • Google's advertising policies and choices: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. If you are in one of these regions, the game shows Google's consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalised. You can accept or refuse personalised ads. If you refuse, you may still see ads that are not personalised, which still use some of the data above for limited purposes such as frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings (privacy choices).

Your controls on Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your phone's settings (usually Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

3. Notifications

If you allow it, the game can remind you about the daily challenge and your daily reward. These reminders are scheduled on your phone by Android and are not sent from a server. No data is sent to us or to anyone else to make them work. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in Android's notification settings at any time.

4. Sharing and friend challenges

When you tap Share (for example, to share a picture of your score or a challenge code), the game opens your phone's system share sheet and you choose the app to share with. The game only shares what you see: the picture or the text with the code. A challenge code contains only a number that sets the order of the pieces and your score; it contains no name or other personal information. Whatever you share is then handled by the app you chose, under its own privacy policy. We do not receive a copy.

Challenge links (blokari://) open the game on the receiving phone and are read on that device only.

5. What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location or any account details.
  • This version of the game has no analytics and sends no crash reports to us.
  • In-app purchases: none in this version. Nothing can be bought, and no payment information is handled.
  • The game asks for only two Android permissions: to show notifications, and to restore scheduled reminders after your phone restarts. It also uses internet access for ads.

6. Children

Blokari is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game itself collects no personal information from anyone. If you believe a child under 13 is using the game in a way that has resulted in data being collected through ads, please contact us and we will help, including by pointing you to Google's tools.

7. Security and retention

Data stored on your device is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read, until you clear it or uninstall the game. Data collected by Google through ads is sent over encrypted connections and kept according to Google's own retention policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EEA and UK), you have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Because we do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access or delete on our side; your on-device data can be deleted as described in section 1. For data collected by Google for ads, you can use the controls in section 2 or contact Google. You can also contact us and we will help you with your request.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your information (for example, if we add analytics, crash reporting or in-app purchases), we will update this policy and the effective date above before that version is released.
