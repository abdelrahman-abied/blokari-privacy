Blokari Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you play Blokari (package com.amacraft.blokari), a puzzle game for Android made by amacraft ("we", "us").

In short

  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device. We have no server and no account system, and your saved game is not sent to us.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show and measure ads, as described below. In the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent first.
  • The game uses Google Firebase for crash reports and usage analytics (for example, the mode and score of a finished game). This data is linked to random identifiers for your installation, never to your name. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics follows the choice you make in the consent form.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Sharing only happens when you tap Share, through your phone's own share sheet.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following in its private storage on your phone, so you can continue where you left off:

  • scores, best scores, your saved game, levels, stars, puzzles solved, achievements and stickers;
  • coins, power-ups, unlocked themes, missions, daily rewards, streaks and season progress;
  • the recent friend challenges you sent or opened (the number that sets the piece order, the scores and the date);
  • your settings: language, light or dark mode, sound, colour marks, left-handed layout and reminder preferences.

This saved information is not sent to us. Only the few game figures described in section 3 (such as the score of a finished game) are sent to Google Analytics, as separate events. You can delete all of the saved information by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the game. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this data in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account, and we cannot access it.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

Blokari is free and is paid for by ads: banners, full-screen ads between games, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for coins or other in-game items. The ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited in the EEA and UK).

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game collects and sends to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (city or region, not your precise location);
  • device and app information, such as the device model, operating system, language and app version;
  • interactions with ads, such as ads shown, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information about how the app and the ad SDK perform.

We do not see this data in a form that identifies you; we only receive summary reports from AdMob (for example, how many ads were shown). Google uses this data as described in its policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google's advertising policies and choices: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. If you are in one of these regions, the game shows Google's consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalised. You can accept or refuse personalised ads. If you refuse, you may still see ads that are not personalised, which still use some of the data above for limited purposes such as frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings (privacy choices).

Your controls on Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your phone's settings (usually Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

3. Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and to fix crashes, the game uses two Google Firebase services: Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. Firebase is provided by Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited in the EEA and UK), which processes this data for us as a service provider.

Analytics. The game sends only two events of its own:

  • when a game ends: the game mode, the score, the number of lines cleared, whether you won, and whether you continued the game;
  • when you use a power-up: the game mode and the name of the power-up.

Google Analytics also collects some information automatically: an app instance ID (a random identifier for this installation of the game), your device's advertising ID where Android makes it available, the first time the app is opened, sessions, screens viewed and time spent in the app, app updates, the device model, operating system, language and app version, and your country or general region, estimated from your IP address (not your precise location). We see this data only in Google Analytics reports and use it to understand which modes and features are played, so we can improve the game.

Crash reports. If the game crashes or hits an unexpected error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report to Google: the technical details of the error (the stack trace), the device model, operating system version, app version, the time of the crash and a Crashlytics installation ID (a random identifier for this installation). We use crash reports only to find and fix bugs.

Neither service receives your name, email address, precise location, or anything you type or share.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. The Google consent form described in section 2 also sets Google's consent mode for Analytics, so whether Analytics may store and use its identifiers follows your choice. If you refuse, Analytics does not store or use them, and Google receives only limited information without these identifiers. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings (privacy choices). Outside these regions, Analytics is on by default.

Your controls. You can reset the app instance ID and the Crashlytics installation ID at any time by clearing the game's storage in Android settings (this also deletes your saved progress, see section 1) or by uninstalling the game. You can also reset your advertising ID in Android settings, as described in section 2.

Retention. Analytics data is kept according to Google Analytics' data retention setting, which we have set to 2 months; after that, data linked to the app instance ID is deleted and only summary reports remain. Firebase keeps crash reports and their installation IDs for 90 days.

  • Firebase privacy and security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Notifications

If you allow it, the game can remind you about the daily challenge and your daily reward. These reminders are scheduled on your phone by Android and are not sent from a server. No data is sent to us or to anyone else to make them work. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in Android's notification settings at any time.

5. Sharing and friend challenges

When you tap Share (for example, to share a picture of your score or a challenge code), the game opens your phone's system share sheet and you choose the app to share with. The game only shares what you see: the picture or the text with the code. A challenge code contains only a number that sets the order of the pieces and your score; it contains no name or other personal information. Whatever you share is then handled by the app you chose, under its own privacy policy. We do not receive a copy.

Challenge links (blokari://) open the game on the receiving phone and are read on that device only.

6. What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location or any account details.
  • In-app purchases: none in this version. Nothing can be bought, and no payment information is handled.
  • The game asks for only two Android permissions: to show notifications, and to restore scheduled reminders after your phone restarts. It also uses internet access for ads, analytics and crash reports.

7. Children

Blokari is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game does not ask anyone for personal information. If you believe a child under 13 is using the game in a way that has resulted in data being collected through ads, analytics or crash reports, please contact us and we will help, including by pointing you to Google's tools.

8. Security and retention

Data stored on your device is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read, until you clear it or uninstall the game. Data collected by Google through ads, analytics and crash reports is sent over encrypted connections. Ads data is kept according to Google's own retention policies; analytics and crash reports are kept as described in section 3.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EEA and UK), you have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. We do not hold your name or any contact details. The analytics and crash data in section 3 are linked only to random identifiers for your installation, which we cannot connect to you. Your on-device data can be deleted as described in section 1. For data collected by Google for ads, you can use the controls in section 2; for analytics and crash reports, the controls in section 3. You can also contact Google, or contact us and we will help you with your request.

10. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your information (for example, if we add in-app purchases or leaderboards), we will update this policy and the effective date above before that version is released.
