Blokari Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you play Blokari (package com.amacraft.blokari), a puzzle game for Android made by amacraft ("we", "us").

In short

  • Your game progress and settings are stored on your device. You can play the whole game without an account.
  • Cloud save is optional. If you turn it on in the game's settings, you sign in with your Google account and a copy of your progress is kept in Google Firebase, so you can get it back on another phone. Only you can open it from the game, it is never used for ads, and you can delete it at any time, in the game or by email (how to delete your account).
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show and measure ads, as described below. In the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent first.
  • The game uses Google Firebase for crash reports and usage analytics (for example, the mode and score of a finished game). This data is linked to random identifiers for your installation, never to your name or your account. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics follows the choice you make in the consent form. At launch the game also downloads game settings (such as prices and event dates) from Firebase.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Sharing only happens when you tap Share, through your phone's own share sheet.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following in its private storage on your phone, so you can continue where you left off:

  • scores, best scores, your saved game, levels, stars, puzzles solved, achievements and stickers;
  • coins, power-ups, unlocked themes, missions, daily rewards, streaks and season progress;
  • the recent friend challenges you sent or opened (the number that sets the piece order, the scores and the date);
  • your settings: language, light or dark mode, sound, colour marks, left-handed layout and reminder preferences.

If you add the Blokari widget to your home screen, it shows your streak, today's challenge and your best score, read from this same data on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere to show it.

This saved information is not sent to us. Only the few game figures described in section 3 (such as the score of a finished game) are sent to Google Analytics, as separate events. If you turn on cloud save, a copy of this information is also kept in your cloud save, as described in section 4; that is optional and off unless you choose it. You can delete all of the information on your phone by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the game. This does not delete a cloud save (see section 4). If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this data in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account, and we cannot access it.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

Blokari is free and is paid for by ads: banners, full-screen ads between games, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for coins or other in-game items. The ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited in the EEA and UK).

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game collects and sends to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (city or region, not your precise location);
  • device and app information, such as the device model, operating system, language and app version;
  • interactions with ads, such as ads shown, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information about how the app and the ad SDK perform.

We do not see this data in a form that identifies you; we only receive summary reports from AdMob (for example, how many ads were shown). Google uses this data as described in its policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google's advertising policies and choices: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. If you are in one of these regions, the game shows Google's consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalised. You can accept or refuse personalised ads. If you refuse, you may still see ads that are not personalised, which still use some of the data above for limited purposes such as frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings (privacy choices).

Your controls on Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your phone's settings (usually Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

3. Analytics, crash reports and game settings (Google Firebase)

To understand how the game is played, to fix crashes and to adjust the game without an update, the game uses three Google Firebase services: Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Remote Config. Firebase is provided by Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited in the EEA and UK), which processes this data for us as a service provider.

Analytics. The game sends only these events of its own:

  • when a game ends: the game mode, the score, the number of lines cleared, whether you won, and whether you continued the game;
  • when you use a power-up: the game mode and the name of the power-up;
  • if you use cloud save (section 4): that you signed in, that your progress was restored, or that it was uploaded. These three events carry no details at all: no email address, no name and no user ID.

Google Analytics also collects some information automatically: an app instance ID (a random identifier for this installation of the game), your device's advertising ID where Android makes it available, the first time the app is opened, sessions, screens viewed and time spent in the app, app updates, the device model, operating system, language and app version, and your country or general region, estimated from your IP address (not your precise location). We see this data only in Google Analytics reports and use it to understand which modes and features are played, so we can improve the game.

Game settings (Remote Config). When the game starts, it downloads game settings that we set in Firebase, such as prices, rewards and event dates, so we can change them without an app update. To do this, the Firebase SDK sends Google a Firebase installation ID (a random identifier for this installation of the game), the app's ID and version, and your device's language, country, time zone and operating system version. No personal data is sent. Google keeps installation IDs according to Firebase's own retention rules.

Crash reports. If the game crashes or hits an unexpected error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report to Google: the technical details of the error (the stack trace), the device model, operating system version, app version, the time of the crash and a Crashlytics installation ID (a random identifier for this installation). We use crash reports only to find and fix bugs.

None of these three services receives your name, email address, precise location, or anything you type or share.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. The Google consent form described in section 2 also sets Google's consent mode for Analytics, so whether Analytics may store and use its identifiers follows your choice. If you refuse, Analytics does not store or use them, and Google receives only limited information without these identifiers. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings (privacy choices). Outside these regions, Analytics is on by default.

Your controls. You can reset the app instance ID, the Crashlytics installation ID and the Firebase installation ID at any time by clearing the game's storage in Android settings (this also deletes the progress on your phone, see section 1) or by uninstalling the game. You can also reset your advertising ID in Android settings, as described in section 2.

Retention. Analytics data is kept according to Google Analytics' data retention setting, which we have set to 2 months; after that, data linked to the app instance ID is deleted and only summary reports remain. Firebase keeps crash reports and their installation IDs for 90 days.

  • Firebase privacy and security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Cloud save (optional)

Cloud save keeps a copy of your progress with your Google account, so you can get it back on a new phone or after reinstalling the game. It is off unless you turn it on yourself in the game's settings (Cloud save > "Sign in with Google"). The game never asks you to sign in when it starts, and you can play the whole game without it. Cloud save is available on Android.

What is stored. When you turn it on, you sign in with your Google account through Firebase Authentication, and two things are kept:

  • Your account in Firebase Authentication: the email address, name and profile photo link that Google provides, your Google account ID, a Firebase user ID, and the dates the account was created and last signed in. Google's Firebase documentation says that Firebase Authentication also records the IP address used to sign in, to protect sign-in against abuse; Google keeps it according to Firebase's own retention rules. The game shows your email address on its settings screen, so you can see which account you are using; it does not show your name or photo anywhere.
  • Your cloud save in Cloud Firestore: a copy of the information on your device described in section 1 (scores, levels, stars, coins, themes, missions, streaks, season, achievements and similar, and some of your settings), with the time it was saved and the app version. It is one record, linked to your Firebase user ID. It contains no email address or name: the database's security rules refuse any field other than these.

Why. Only to restore your progress. Your account and cloud save are not used for ads or analytics, are not shared with anyone, and are not sold. Google provides Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore as a service provider for us, as in section 3.

When it is sent. The game uploads your progress when you leave it (when it goes to the background) and after a game ends, over encrypted connections.

Who can see it. Only you, while signed in, can read or change your cloud save: the database's security rules refuse every other player and app. Google stores it for us. As the developer, we can see the account list and the saved records in the Firebase console; we look at them only to handle a request from you, such as a deletion.

Where it is stored. Google stores your account and cloud save in the United States (Cloud Firestore's nam5 multi-region). If you live elsewhere, for example in the EEA or the UK, this means your data is transferred abroad. These transfers are covered by Google's data processing terms for Firebase, which include the standard contractual clauses:

  • Firebase privacy and security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Firebase data processing terms: https://firebase.google.com/terms/data-processing-terms

How long it is kept. Until you delete it. Signing out in the game stops saving to the cloud and keeps both copies, the one on your phone and the one in the cloud. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage does not delete your cloud save.

How to delete it.

  • In the game: Settings > Cloud save > Delete my cloud save. This deletes your cloud save and your account at once. The progress on your phone stays.
  • Without the app: email amacraft.io@gmail.com from the address of the Google account you signed in with, and ask us to delete your account. We delete your account and cloud save within 30 days and reply to confirm.

The same steps, and exactly what is deleted, are on our account deletion page: Delete your Blokari account.

5. Notifications

If you allow it, the game can remind you about the daily challenge and your daily reward. These reminders are scheduled on your phone by Android and are not sent from a server. No data is sent to us or to anyone else to make them work. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in Android's notification settings at any time.

6. Sharing and friend challenges

When you tap Share (for example, to share a picture of your score or a challenge code), the game opens your phone's system share sheet and you choose the app to share with. The game only shares what you see: the picture or the text with the code. A challenge code contains only a number that sets the order of the pieces and your score; it contains no name or other personal information. Whatever you share is then handled by the app you chose, under its own privacy policy. We do not receive a copy.

Challenge links (blokari://) open the game on the receiving phone and are read on that device only.

7. What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for or collect your phone number, contacts, photos, files or precise location.
  • Your email address, name and Google account details are collected only if you turn on cloud save, only to run your account and restore your progress, and never for ads or analytics (section 4). Without cloud save, we do not collect them.
  • In-app purchases: none in this version. Nothing can be bought, and no payment information is handled.
  • The game asks for only two Android permissions: to show notifications, and to restore scheduled reminders after your phone restarts. It also uses internet access for ads, analytics, crash reports, game settings and cloud save.

8. Children

Blokari is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game does not ask anyone for personal information to play; only the optional cloud save uses a Google account. If you believe a child under 13 is using the game in a way that has resulted in data being collected through ads, analytics, crash reports or cloud save, please contact us and we will help, including by deleting their account and cloud save and by pointing you to Google's tools.

9. Security and retention

Data stored on your device is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read, until you clear it or uninstall the game. Everything the game sends to Google for ads, analytics, crash reports, game settings and cloud save travels over encrypted connections. The database's security rules let only you, while signed in, read or change your cloud save. It is kept until you delete it, and if you ask us by email, we delete it within 30 days (section 4). Ads data is kept according to Google's own retention policies; analytics, crash reports and installation IDs are kept as described in section 3.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EEA and UK), you have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing.

If you use cloud save, we hold your account and cloud save (section 4), and you can use these rights for them:

  • Access: email amacraft.io@gmail.com from your account's address, and we will send you a copy of your account details and your cloud save.
  • Correction: your email address, name and photo come from your Google account; change them there.
  • Deletion: delete your account and cloud save in the game, or by email, as described in section 4 and on our account deletion page.

If you do not use cloud save, we do not hold your name or any contact details. The analytics and crash data in section 3 are linked only to random identifiers for your installation, which we cannot connect to you or to your account. Your on-device data can be deleted as described in section 1. For data collected by Google for ads, you can use the controls in section 2; for analytics and crash reports, the controls in section 3. You can also contact Google, or contact us and we will help you with your request.

11. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your information (for example, if we add in-app purchases or leaderboards, or keep more in your cloud save), we will update this policy and the effective date above before that version is released.
